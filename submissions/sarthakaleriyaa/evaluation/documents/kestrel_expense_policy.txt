KESTREL LABS - TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY
(Fictional policy written for the DocuMind evaluation set. Free to share.)

PURPOSE
This policy explains which business expenses Kestrel Labs reimburses and how to
claim them. It applies to all employees and contractors travelling on company
business.

SUBMITTING CLAIMS
Expense claims must be submitted in the finance tool within 30 days of the
expense date. Claims submitted later than 30 days are only reimbursed with
written approval from the finance director. Every claim above 25 EUR needs an
itemised receipt. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run.

TRAVEL
Train travel is preferred for journeys under 4 hours. Flights must be booked in
economy class; business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours.
Hotel bookings are reimbursed up to 160 EUR per night in most cities and up to
220 EUR per night in London, Paris, New York and Tokyo.

MEALS
While travelling, employees receive a daily meal allowance of 45 EUR. Alcohol is
not reimbursed, except at client dinners approved in advance by a director.

MILEAGE
Use of a private car for business trips is reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre.
Parking and tolls are reimbursed with receipts. Traffic fines are never
reimbursed.

CORPORATE CARDS
Employees who travel more than six times a year may request a corporate card.
Personal purchases on a corporate card are not allowed and must be repaid within
14 days.
