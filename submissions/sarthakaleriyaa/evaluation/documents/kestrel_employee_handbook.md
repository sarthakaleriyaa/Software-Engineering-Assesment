# Kestrel Labs Employee Handbook

Kestrel Labs is a fictional company. This handbook was written for the DocuMind
evaluation set and may be shared freely.

## 1. Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone.
Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedule freely as long as the
standard 40-hour week is met. Meetings should not be scheduled on Friday
afternoons, which are reserved for focused work.

## 2. Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in
addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their
contracted hours. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the first quarter
of the next year; carried-over days that are not used by 31 March expire.

Leave requests of more than 3 consecutive days must be submitted in the HR portal
at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by the employee's manager.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees should notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of illness.
A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working
days. Sick leave does not reduce the annual leave allowance.

## 4. Parental leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which
can be taken at any time within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## 5. Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require written approval from the department head. Employees working remotely
from another country for more than 30 days in a year must inform HR first, because
of tax and insurance rules.

The company provides a one-time home-office allowance of 600 EUR for equipment
such as a desk, chair or monitor.

## 6. Learning and development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 EUR for courses,
conferences and books. Unused budget does not roll over. Requests above 300 EUR
need manager approval before booking.

## 7. Probation and notice

The probation period is 6 months. During probation, either side may end the
employment with 2 weeks' notice. After probation, the notice period is 1 month
for both sides, ending at the end of a calendar month.
