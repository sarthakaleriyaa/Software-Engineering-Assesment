# Kestrel T200 Smart Thermostat — User Manual

The Kestrel T200 is a fictional product. This manual was written for the DocuMind
evaluation set and may be shared freely.

## Box contents

The box contains the T200 thermostat, a wall plate, four screws, a 12-wire
terminal label sheet and a quick-start card. Batteries are not included because
the T200 is powered by the heating system's 24 V C-wire.

## Installation

Switch off power to the heating system at the breaker before removing the old
thermostat. Label each wire with the supplied stickers, mount the wall plate,
connect the wires and attach the T200. Installation usually takes about 30
minutes. If your system has no C-wire, use the optional Kestrel Power Adapter
(sold separately).

## Connecting to Wi-Fi

The T200 supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks only; 5 GHz networks are not supported.
Open the Kestrel Home app, tap "Add device" and follow the instructions. The
thermostat shows a blinking blue light while it is in pairing mode.

## Schedules and eco mode

You can create up to 8 schedule blocks per day. Eco mode lowers the target
temperature to 17 °C when everyone has left home, using the phones' location.
The default temperature range is 5 °C to 30 °C.

## Resetting the device

To restart the T200, press and hold the dial for 10 seconds. To perform a
factory reset, press and hold the dial for 30 seconds until the ring turns red;
this deletes all schedules and Wi-Fi settings.

## Error codes

E1 means the temperature sensor has failed; contact support.
E3 means no power on the C-wire; check the breaker and the wiring.
E7 means the Wi-Fi password was rejected; re-enter the password in the app.

## Warranty

The T200 has a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty
does not cover damage caused by incorrect wiring or by water.
