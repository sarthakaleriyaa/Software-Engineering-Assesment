KESTREL LABS - IT SECURITY POLICY
Fictional policy written for the DocuMind evaluation set. Free to share.

1. Scope
This policy applies to every laptop, phone and cloud account used for Kestrel Labs work, including personal devices that access company email.

2. Device security
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within 7 days of release.
=====
3. Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Password reuse across services is not allowed, and every employee must use the company password manager. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, source code hosting and the HR portal. Hardware security keys are issued to administrators.

4. Reporting incidents
Suspected security incidents, including lost devices and phishing emails, must be reported to security@kestrel.example within 1 hour of discovery. Do not try to investigate an incident yourself.
=====
5. Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer payment details, may only be stored in approved systems and must never be sent by email.

6. Access reviews
Managers review their team's access rights every 90 days. Accounts of employees who leave the company are disabled on their last working day.
